Chain of command And Staff of the week

Staff of the week is a speical role given to a new person each week. Staff of the week works by the BODs choosing a member for the role. If you get the role 3 times you will win Robux or a Tier.

If you want to earn this you have to be active in game and be active in Discord.

The chain of command is the order that the ranks go. You have to follow the chain of command. That means when a lower rank can do something don't go asking the higher rank to do that. Below is the COC

Awaiting Training

No special permissions

Must complete training within one week of being hired.

Moderation Team

Can moderate in game

Mod permissions in game

Can create warnings, kick, and ban BOLOS with Melony

Can do :h Command

Administration Team

All Moderation team permissions.

Can ban in game players and log it with Melony

Can handle General support tickets

Can give permission for Roadwork Roleplay

Administrators+ will receive the Training Officer role. (Must be supervised by a fellow Training Officer for the first training that you host)

Can start a Peace timer (max time is 300)

Junior Admin+ Can go on 50/50 shifts with SHR+ permission

Management Team

All Internal Affairs permissions

Can do :m command in game

Moderate the discord server

Read applications from potential staff members

Can handle Management support tickets

Can ban members from the Discord

Able to reinstate staff members.

Internal Affairs Team

All the Administration team perms

Can issue infractions to all staff members

Can handle IA tickets

Can give permissions for all the priority roleplays

Can give mod permissions in game

Can kick/mute/warn members in the discord

Board of Directors

All Community Manager permissions

Can promote staff

Have access to all the Discord channels

Can host sessions

Constantly working to improve the server
