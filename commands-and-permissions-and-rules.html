Commands and Permissions and Rules

There are some permission and commands that only certain ranks can use. Please make sure that you do not violate these rules. If you do punishments will be given.

  • NEVER use the all / others command.
  • The peacetime command is only allowed for TA+ with the max time being 300s
  • If you kick a player, they must wait a minimum of 30 minutes before joining back. You have to say this to the user.
  • Only Management+ can use the :m command
  • Users that are not in the Discord must join. You have to say that they have to join. If they refuse you have to kick them.
  • You HAVE to use 4 or more characters of someone's username while executing a command
  • You cannot moderate fellow staff members, you HAVE to open a IA ticket and show proof. This only applies if you are not a HR. If you are a HR make sure you have proof and punish the staff member.
  • Priority's must be enforced.
  • Please be respectful to all members of Iowa State. Any Homophobia or racism, Discrimination, or any toxic behavior towards any member will NOT be tolerated.
  • Every staff member is expected to behave in a fairly professional manner within Iowa State.
  • Staff cannot be invisible/offline on Discord when they are on duty. If not, you will have moderation actions towards you.
  • You must be on duty in Melony when on duty in the server.
  • You cannot shift grind
  • Staff are not expected to ping other staff members unless it is emergency
  • Do not train people unless you have the Training officer role.
  • Staff must use English at all times
  • Staff are expected to use SPaG (Spelling, Punctuation, And grammar) everywhere. This is not required in DM's or if you do you :pm message to a staff member. You have to use SPaG if the person is not staff.
  • Spam answering to calls is not allowed.
  • The max break time is 10 minutes
  • Do not use commands off duty unless you are the proper rank
  • You cannot teleport or bring HR+ without permission
  • You must have a realistic avatar at all times
  • Do not moderate other staff members; file an Internal Affairs ticket if needed.
  • HGMGT+ are allowed to use :loadlayout

Failure to follow these rules will result in punishments. They have different punishments ranging from warnings to Termination

Discord Regulations

  • You have to set your Discord nickname in this format(Rank)|(callsign)|(Roblox username)For example O | 250-hp | Youmo_official. You can also have other department's such as media team in the callsign place. Having you rank and Roblox username is a priority

The callsign is only for departments callsigns. Please do not use your staff callsign in the callsign space .
