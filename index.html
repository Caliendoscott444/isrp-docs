Introduction

Welcome to the Iowa State Roleplay Staff team!

Your role is to help create a safe, enjoyable, and immersive roleplay environment for everyone in the community.

As a staff member is it important to read over this Handbook. Also read through all the resources linked below. They'll guide you through everything you need to know.

Core Qualities We Value:
• Respectful communication
• Strong problem-solving skills
• Clear and proper grammar
• Friendly and approachable with all age groups
• Kindness and professionalism
