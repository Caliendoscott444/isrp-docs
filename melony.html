Melony

At Iowa State Roleplay we use a website called Melony to log moderation, log shifts of staff members, handle priority requests, and control Leave of Absences.

Logging Moderations

To create a moderation log on Melony you can either use the in game command :log (type) (user) (reason) OR you can create a log on the website by clicking the Create New Log on the home screen. If you are using the website you can also select a preset. This is highly encouraged

Creating Priority Requests

To approve a priority you have to click on the Priority Request button on the Melony Dashboard and then reviewing the Pending requests and either accepting them or denying them.
Priority must be enforced; if someone is robbing a bank or jewelry store, starting high-speed pursuits, holding a hostage, please ensure they have either booster perks or they may have perms. Once given perms, remove the priority timer.

You have to be a HR+ to approve Priority's. And the user HAS to make the Priority request not you.

Leave of Absences

If you are unable to reach your weekly quota for any reason, please make a LOA using the LOA request form

Your loa request must be submitted before Tuesday of the current week to be accepted. After Tuesday, you will need to open a ticket and explain why you were unable to submit it prior to the delay. After Tuesday, your LOA will be denied if the reason is not strong enough.


Shift Management

Use the Melony Dashboard and click on Start Shift. There are many types of shifts that you can choose one. It is REALLY IMPORTANT that you use the correct shift type. What the shift types are for are listed below. You may also go on break for no longer than 5 minutes.

Some of these shifts have your time being reduced or increased per second. For example, 50/50 shift counts for 0.5x of your quota. That means if you get 1 hour of shift time, it only counts for 30 minutes towards your quota.

Default

This is the shift type that you go on if your just going on duty with nothing special.

This shift counts for 1x of your time.

50/50

This shift type allows you to Roleplay on duty! You HAVE to have permission to be on this shift type. Only Supervisory Team+ can give out 50/50 permission.

This shift type counts for 0.5x of your shift time.

50/50 shift is only active for 3 hours after it was accepted

Administrative Tasks

This is the shift that you go on if you are not actively moderating but helping the server (Excluding alting.) For example if you are making livery's you go on this shift type.

This shift type counts for 0.5x of your shift time

Session Perm

This is the shift that you go on if you are alting. You can only go on this shift type if you have the Session Perm role

This shift type counts for 1.25x of your shift time.

Quota boost

This is the shift type that you go on if Quota boost is active. You can check if the quota booster is active by looking in the Quota boost channel under the Staff information category.

This shift type counts for 1.25x of your shift time.

Training

This is the shift type that you go on if you are training a future staff member. You have to have the training officer role to go on this shift type.

This shift type counts for 1.25x of your shift time.

Server Designer

This is the shift type that you go on if you are working on projects that benefit the server, including but not limited to liverys, uniforms, bots, and other approved server related work. You have to have the server designer role to go on this shift type and must screen share what you are doing in a VC.

This shift type counts for 0.5x of your shift time.
