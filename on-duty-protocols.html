On duty protocols

While on duty there are protocols that you have to follow. if you do not follow these you will be punished.

There are 8 cars that you can choose from that have staff livery's. Some of which may say HR only. If you are not a HR only do not use them. The staff cars are listed below with the requirements that you need to get them.

Bullhorn Prancer Pursuit 2015

Requires 500 XP/Deputy+ in Law Enforcement

Bullhorn Determinator SFP Fury (all trims)

Requires 4000 XP/Lieutenant+ in Law Enforcement

2014 Chevlon Corbeta RZR

Requires 13500 XP/Colonel +in Law Enforcement

Falcon stallion 350

Requires 49,000 dollars to buy.

Bullhorn Prancer Pursuit Widebody 2020

Requires 9000 XP/Major+ in Law Enforcement

Chevlon Camion PPV 2018

Requires $34,500 in game cash

Bullhorn Pueblo Pursuit 2022

Requires 6,500 XP

Falcon eStallion 2024

Costs $55,900 and the Electric vehicle gamepass

You also MUST use the staff uniform that corresponds to your rank in Discord. For example, if you are a moderator, you must wear the moderation team uniform.

Guns are NOT allowed on duty.

If you are on 50/50 shift you can have a gun. Make sure it's for roleplay purposes only.

We would also like to remind you that when you are on duty, you have several tasks: responding to calls, performing Discord verifications by using the command /erlc membercheck or /erlc vehiclecheck, or patrolling, all in this order! Unless you are in a 50/50, you cannot engage in roleplay.

DO NOT park on the side of the road if you have to go AFK. Please make sure to find a parking space around the ERLC map. You can park on the side of the road to answer mod calls.

If you go AFK on duty make sure to be on break

When you join the Staff team make sure your callsign is "Rank-1" ,02, 03 etc...

Also, if there is less than 15 players online you CANNOT go on duty.

You may not patrol with another person. This means you cannot have another player that's in your car or you are the one driving the car with another player in.

When on duty, if the player count is below 30 you are not allowed to kick or ban someone. Instead you have to keep on jailing them.

When you are patrolling, please make sure you are on STG 1 ELS.

If you are on 50/50 and someone RDM's you. They should not be kicked for staff RDM. Instead, they should be warned for RDM since they can not identify you are a staff member.
