Punishments

When a user in the Roblox server breaks a rule they should be moderated with either a Temporary Ban, Warning, Kick, Or a ban. Below is a list of rules and what it corresponds to.

Warning

Tool abuse

Random Death Match

Vehicle Death Match

Fail Roleplay

GTA Driving

Lying to staff
(You must give details when you log it on Melonly)

Staff Disrespect
(You must give details when you log it on Melonly)

Cuff rushing

Auto jail

New Life Rule (NLR)

Use of Exotics without being Server Booster (Nitro booster not "Booster Perks")

Arguing with staff

Disrespect
(You must give details when you log it on Melonly)

No Intent to Roleplay

RTAP (Respawn to avoid punishment)

Staff Evasion (First offence)

Interfering with a Mod Scene
(You must give him a verbal warning first, then load the player and if he comes back you can give a warning.)

Kick

Mass Random Death Match

Staff (or Mass) Random Death Match

Refusing to change car livery or uniform

Refusing to join Discord

Trolling (Requires details)

Pretending to be staff

3x Warnings
(Must be in the same session)

Staff Evasion (Second offence)

Temporary Ban

Leave to Avoid Punishment
Accidental glitching

(3 day temp ban: When you log it on Melonly you must select the temp ban option... no proof required. Also you must put the action that the player did before they LTAPed.)

Ban (Ban bolo) (proof required to be added to Melonly when you log it)

Racism

Rejoining after kick

(Must be in the same session)
