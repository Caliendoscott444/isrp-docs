Quota, Promotions, Punishments, And Retirements.

Here at Iowa State staff are required to have a certain amount of hours a week based on your rank. Failure to meet your quota will result in a punishment. The amount of hours per rank is listed below. The quota resets every Sunday unless said otherwise. You may have more hours than what is listed.

The quota depends on what team you are. It ONLY changes once you change a team.

TeamIn game quotaTicket quota
Moderation team 2 Hours None
Administration team2 Hours2 Tickets
Management Team3 Hours4 Tickets

Promotions Demotions Punishments and Retirement

Promotions are given to staff members based on their activity and performance (maturity) in and out of the game. Promotions can happen any time we feel like it is needed.

To see promotions hours requirement run the command /see required quota in staff command channel.

Begging for a promotion or arguing about you not getting promoted is not going to get you a promotion. Instead you will get a punishment.

Demotions can happen anytime if we feel like you are not fit for your position. Demotions are not appealable.

When you want to Retire from Iowa State Roleplay please make a Management ticket and someone will retire you.

You can only retire and reinstate maximum of 3 times. If you wish to be staff again after the 3rd time you will have to reapply.

There are 4 types of Punishments you can have. You can get a Warning, Infraction, Strike, and Termination.

If you get a warning, next time you do the same offense it will be a Infraction.

If you get 3 infractions it turns into a strike.

If you get 4 Strikes it will result in a termination which means you are no longer on the staff team.

Strikes and infractions are taken away 5 weeks after you have received them.

You can also be under investigation. If you are all your permissions will be temporary removed. Also, you need permission from TMGMT + to terminate someone if you are IA.
