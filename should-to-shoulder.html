Should to Shoulder

STS or Shoulder to Shoulder is when you teleport all players and make them line up on a line and explain the rules to all of the users. It may also be used for Discord Checks.

Only BOD+ can hosts STS. If you are not BOD and hosting STS you will be punished

During STS, kick players that are trolling, moving excessively, abusing gears or emoting and if they are not in the discord.

ALWAYS check if players are in Discord, especially at the end of each STS

STS do not happen often, they occur because the server is REALLY chaotic
