# Melony

<h4 align="center">At Iowa State Roleplay we use a website called Melony to log moderation, log shifts of staff members, handle priority requests, and control Leave of Absences.</h4>

***

<h3 align="center"><mark style="color:blue;"><strong>Logging Moderations</strong></mark></h3>

To create a moderation log on Melony you can either use the in game command <kbd>:log (type) (user) (reason)</kbd>  **OR** you can create a log on the website by clicking the Create New Log on the home screen. If you are using the website you can also select a preset. **This is highly encouraged**&#x20;

<h4 align="center"><mark style="color:blue;"><strong>Creating Priority Requests</strong></mark></h4>

To approve a priority you have to click on the Priority Request button on the Melony Dashboard and then reviewing the Pending requests and either accepting them or denying them.\
Priority must be enforced; if someone is robbing a bank or jewelry store, starting high-speed pursuits, holding a hostage, please ensure they have either booster perks or they may have perms. Once given perms, remove the priority timer.

You have to be a HR+ to approve Priority's. And the user **HAS** to make the Priority request not you.
{% endhint %}

<h3 align="center"><mark style="color:blue;"><strong>Promotions Demotions Punishments and Retirement</strong></mark> </h3>

Promotions are given to staff members based on their activity and performance (maturity) in and out of the game. Promotions can happen any time we feel like it is needed.&#x20;

To see promotions hours requirement run the command `/see required quota` in staff command channel.

Begging for a promotion or arguing about you not getting promoted is not going to get you a promotion. Instead you will get a punishment.

***

Demotions can happen anytime if we feel like you are not fit for your position. Demotions are not appealable.

***

When you want to Retire from Iowa State Roleplay please make a Management ticket and someone will retire you.

You can only retire and reinstate maximum of 3 times. If you wish to be staff again after the 3rd time you will have to reapply.

***

There are 4 types of Punishments you can have. You can get a Warning, Infraction, Strike, and Termination.

If you get a warning, next time you do the same offense it will be a Infraction.

If you get 3 infractions it turns into a strike.

If you get 4 Strikes it will result in a termination which means you are no longer on the staff team.

Strikes and infractions are taken away 5 weeks after you have received them.

{% hint style="success" %}
You can also be under investigation. If you are all your permissions will be temporary removed. Also, you need permission from TMGMT + to terminate someone if you are IA.
{% endhint %}
