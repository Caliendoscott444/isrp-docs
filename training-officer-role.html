Training Officer Role

A training officer is a person who trains future staff members with the Awaiting Training role. Once done with a training they will accept or deny someone training.

All training officers are expected to read the Training Document. You will find this document in Training Officer Guide channel. Do not share the Training Document with ANYONE. If you do you will be heavily moderated.
